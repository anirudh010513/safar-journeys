Our story

Two people, one Maruti, and a lot of wrong turns

In 2008 we were booking trips for friends out of a one-room office in Bengaluru — manually, on a landline, with a printed list of hotels we'd personally stayed in.

Eighteen years later the method hasn't really changed. We still drive the routes. We still sleep in the properties before they go on a quote. We still know which houseboat operator in Alleppey has the good cook, and which hotel in Leh has rooms that actually face the mountains.

What's changed is scale — a team of 26 across Bengaluru and Kochi, 140 itineraries in the library, and partner hotels and DMCs in 12 countries. What hasn't changed is that you get a name and a number, not a ticket queue.
